From idea to business

How does it actually work?

Seven steps. Each one hands the next everything it already knows, so nothing about your product has to be explained twice.

Start with an idea

  1. 01

    Start with an idea

    Have an idea for a product or an online business? Tell us what you want to build, in your own words. No brief, no spec, no forms.

  2. 02

    Talk to your AI product manager

    It asks the questions a good product manager would ask, understands what you actually mean, and turns the conversation into a detailed product plan.

    How a conversation becomes a commitment

  3. 03

    We build it

    The plan goes to our Guardians, who turn it into a working product. Your first version arrives within 24 hours.

  4. 04

    Keep improving

    Review it, give feedback, ask for changes, keep building. Your entire product context stays with you — every decision, every reason, every version.

    What the record keeps

  5. 05

    Get ready to launch

    When the product is ready, your AI marketing manager learns your audience, your market, your positioning and your goals.

  6. 06

    Create and distribute

    Generate content and go live across X, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Twitch and Kick — from one place.

    How publishing works

  7. 07

    Find your customers

    Everything we already know about your product builds a targeted lead-generation pipeline, connected to the systems you run on.

    The social layer for CRM
